Solar Hybrid Microgrid & Battery Storage EPC

Client: WAPDA (Water & Power Development Authority) Location: Chitral, KP Completed: 2024

Project Overview

Complete EPC implementation of a rugged high-altitude solar microgrid, including structural steel mounting, inverter stations, lithium iron phosphate battery racks, bidirectional synchronization controllers, and distribution feeders.

Engineering Scope & Deliverables

Fescon (Pvt.) Ltd deployed qualified multidisciplinary engineering teams to ensure rigorous execution adhering to Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), IEEE, and IEC technical standards:

  • Comprehensive site surveying, geotechnical load validation, and technical documentation.
  • Turnkey procurement and quality assurance of certified high-voltage and civil construction materials.
  • Erection, installation, testing, and pre-commissioning under stringent Quality and HSE protocols.
  • Final testing, protection relay coordination, calibration, and seamless grid synchronization.
